# 24-electrical data

- Generated by `tools/facts/gen_elec_data.py` from `refs/facts`; do not hand-edit.
- Grades: VERIFIED, DERIVED (named rule), STANDARD, APPROX. `source` is a citation or a rule name.
- Rule name-pairing: L/R and numbered names pair to the same-named bus or unit; graded APPROX.
- Rule panel-by-side: left items in P100/P300/P700, right items in P200/P400/P800; graded APPROX.
- breakers.csv grade: VERIFIED when the sweep grade is NAMED, else APPROX (id only, OCR or check flag).
- loads.csv `unit_rpdu` holds the RPDU when stated, else the panel. Loads on RPDU 61/62 are dropped to open-items.csv.
- Dropped (CONFLICT, EXAMPLE-SCREEN, CHECK) rows are listed in open-items.csv.

## sources.csv
- generators, batteries, RAT, external power receptacles.
- Columns: id, kind, rating, voltage, frequency_range, bus_fed, grade, source.
- Rows: 12; grades: VERIFIED 12

## buses.csv
- AC3, DC and DC+- buses.
- Columns: id, voltage, kind, panel, grade, source.
- Rows: 19; grades: VERIFIED 9, APPROX 10

## conversion.csv
- ATRU, ATU, TRU, SPU, CMSC.
- Columns: id, kind, input_bus, output_bus, rating, grade, source.
- Rows: 19; grades: VERIFIED 6, APPROX 13

## contactors.csv
- named contactors and relays plus CK contactor ids from the breaker sweep (connects blank when unstated).
- Columns: id, name, connects_a, connects_b, panel, type, rating, grade, source.
- Rows: 101; grades: VERIFIED 97, APPROX 4

## breakers.csv
- CE=ECB, CK=ELCF, CT=TCB by id prefix rule.
- Columns: id, name, prefix_type, panel, unit, card_module, channel, rating_a, grade, source, bus, sheddable, dsc_controlled.
- Rows: 284; grades: VERIFIED 183, APPROX 101
- bus: DERIVED from the eps24 table header above the cited row (panel + bus words); ECBs held to DC SPDU buses, side from unit/panel/name.
- sheddable, dsc_controlled: from the eps24 SPDU ECB tables (YES/NO); blank where the table gives none.

## loads.csv
- load to bus, RPDU and breaker path.
- Columns: load, system_ata, voltage_bus, unit_rpdu, breaker_id, rating, draw, grade, source.
- Rows: 1412; grades: VERIFIED 1412

## rpdus.csv
- 17 RPDUs, gateway flag, location (station, buttline, waterline).
- Columns: id, gateway, station, buttline, waterline, location_source, feeds, grade, source.
- Rows: 17; grades: VERIFIED 10, APPROX 7

## open-items.csv
- unresolved, conflicting or dropped rows.
- Columns: file, item, issue, sources.
- Rows: 33
